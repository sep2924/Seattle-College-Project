Privacy Notice

Seattle Central Community is an unofficial, student-run project — not affiliated with Seattle Central College. This page explains what data the app keeps and why, in plain language.

Where your data actually lives right now

This app runs in one of two modes, and it's worth knowing which one you're in:

  • Demo mode (the current default): everything — your account, posts, comments, timer, and schedule — is stored only in your own browser's local storage. Nothing leaves your device, and nothing is shared with other students yet. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • Live mode (once this app is connected to a real backend): your account and anything you post publicly is stored on a shared server so classmates can see it too. This notice will be updated with the specific provider once that's turned on.

What we ask for, and why

  • Email — used only to sign you in. It is never shown to other students.
  • Nickname — what other students see instead of your real name. Pick something that isn't personally identifying.
  • Password — used to sign back in. In demo mode this is only checked against what's stored in your own browser.

What's public vs. private

  • Public to signed-in students: posts, comments, transfer stories, and professor reviews you write, along with your nickname next to them.
  • Private to you only: your study timer history, class schedule, saved stories, and notification settings. These are never shown to anyone else, in either mode.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers. This app has no ads and no third-party trackers.
  • We don't reveal your real name or email to other students — only your nickname is shown on your posts.
  • We don't hand data to Seattle Central College's administration as a matter of course. Reported content may be reviewed by student moderators, as described in the community guidelines.

Removing your data

In demo mode, clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes everything immediately. Once live mode is active, this section will be updated with how to request account deletion from the shared backend.

This is a small, evolving student project, not a legal document drafted by counsel. If something here seems wrong or you have a specific concern, please treat this as a best-effort explanation rather than a binding agreement.
